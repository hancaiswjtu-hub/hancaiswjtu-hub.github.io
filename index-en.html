01 / ABOUT

Rigorous theory
for real-world data challenges

Han Cai is a professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Information Science and Technology, Southwest Jiaotong University, and a recipient of a national young talent award.

His research focuses on coding theory and its applications in distributed storage, wireless communications, and data security. His work has appeared in leading journals including IEEE Transactions on Information Theory and IEEE Transactions on Communications, as well as international conferences such as IEEE ISIT.

He conducted collaborative research at the University of Tsukuba in Japan and later pursued postdoctoral research at Ben-Gurion University of the Negev in Israel. He joined Southwest Jiaotong University in 2021.
